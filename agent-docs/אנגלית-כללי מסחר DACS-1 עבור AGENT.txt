# Strategy Core Definition (DACS-1)
- This is a MONTHLY diagonal/calendar-oriented strategy using monthly expirations.
- Goal: Secure a valid risk-to-reward ratio utilizing Backwardation, based on a Positive Vega position (where the Long leg benefits from IV expansion).


# Step-by-Step Execution Protocol


## Step 1: Direction & Asset Filters
- Default side: CALL. Build the strategy on the Call side unless the user explicitly requested the PUT side.
- Open Interest (OI): Ensure OI > 500 for both legs to guarantee high liquidity.
- Implied Volatility (IV): Target IV between 25% to 30% (Acceptable up to 35% if other parameters are optimal).


## Step 2: Short Leg Selection
- Expiration: Choose the nearest Standard Monthly Expiration (Third Friday of the month). Days to Expiration (DTE) should ideally be between 28 to 45 days.
- Strike Selection by Delta:
  - Standard Condition: Target Delta between 0.10 and 0.20. Aim as close as possible to Delta 0.15 (or slightly less). Absolute maximum Delta allowed is 0.20.
  - Earnings Condition: If the company reports Earnings during the Short leg's cycle, reduce risk -> Target Delta close to 0.10 (Acceptable down to 0.08 if it is an exceptional setup, but no lower than 0.08).
- Expected Move (EM) Context: Calculate Expected Move if available. Note that for monthly cycles, the selected strike may fall near or within the EM boundary; prioritize the Delta constraints above.


## Step 3: Long Leg Selection
- Expiration: The next Standard Monthly Expiration immediately following the Short leg's expiration (+1 month cycle).
- Strike Selection (Margin requirement): Choose a strike that results in a buying power reduction (Margin/Collateral) between $500 to $1,000. (Absolute maximum allowed for an exceptional setup is $1,500).


## Step 4: Position Math & Risk/Reward Filters
Calculate and validate the following metrics for the combined position:


1. Premium Cost/Gain (Credit vs. Debit Decision):
   - Priority: Entry with a CREDIT is highly preferred. The Credit should be between 2% to 4% of the Required Margin.
   - Exception Rule: If the Delta is very low (e.g., during Earnings) and the potential Credit falls below 1.5% of the Margin (making it highly unprofitable), look for a DEBIT setup instead. 
   - Debit Limit: If a Debit is required, it must be as low as possible, with an absolute maximum of $60 per CONTRACT (multiply per-share cost by 100).
     * Each options contract = 100 shares. Example: $0.60/share = $60/contract ✅ Valid. $1.50/share = $150/contract ❌ Invalid.


2. Bid-Ask Spread (Liquidity Check):
   - The Bid-Ask spread for each individual leg must be tight: 3-5 cents for lower-priced stocks, maximum 10 cents for high-priced or high-premium monthly contracts.


3. Fast Ratio Formula:
   - For Credit Positions: Fast Ratio = (Mid Long Cost - Credit) / Margin
   - For Debit Positions: Fast Ratio = (Mid Long Cost + Debit) / Margin
   * Express the result as a percentage (X%).


4. Term Structure:
   - Identify if the position is in Backwardation (highly preferred) or Contango.
